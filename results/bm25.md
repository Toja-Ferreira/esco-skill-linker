# BM25 baseline

Split `test`, 608 occupation-description queries against all 13,939 skills.
Binary metrics score essential relations only; nDCG uses the graded scale (essential 2, optional 1).
Intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap over queries.

## Recall ceilings

Occupations carry ~22 essential skills on average, so only a fraction can fit in the top k. These are the highest recall@k values any system could reach on this split, and recall figures below should be read against them.

| k | 1 | 3 | 5 | 10 | 20 | 50 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| max recall@k | 0.056 | 0.167 | 0.278 | 0.543 | 0.856 | 0.993 |
