# Figure sources

Generated by `python -m experiments.render_docs`; do not edit. Every number in `README.md`, `results/README.md` and `data/README.md` is rendered from one of the figures below. A **lookup** reads one cell of a committed file, a **formula** is computed from other figures at full precision (`{name}` stands for a figure), a **computed** figure is an aggregate over a committed per-query file, and a **constant** cites where it comes from. Constants marked "not reproducible from results/" are recorded nowhere in this repository. Listed: every figure the READMEs use, and every figure those formulas are built from.

The registry is `experiments/figures.py`.

## Notes

- **BM25 baseline source.** Every BM25 figure reads `bm25_stopwords_summary.csv` (experiment 05), which agrees with `bm25_tuned_test.csv [setting=default]`, `dense_vs_lexical.csv` and `residual_gap.csv`. `fusion_test.csv [combination=bm25 pt]` gives 0.265888 rather than 0.265817 for the same configuration. The cause is tie order: `BM25Index.search` and experiment 13's `top_n` both take `np.argpartition` and then a stable sort of the partition, and `argpartition` does not keep corpus order, so skills with equal scores come out in an order that depends on the cut-off. Experiment 05 cuts at 50 and experiment 13 at 200, which reorders the top 10 for 8 of the 608 Portuguese test queries (6 of them have a tie between ranks 10 and 11). Recomputing both from the same scores reproduces 0.265817 at depth 50 and 0.265888 at depth 200; breaking ties by corpus order would give 0.265918. English is unaffected at the fourth decimal.
